YOUR KEYS. YOUR RHYTHM.

Click a key, then press its replacement. Conflicts swap automatically. Escape cancels. Control, Alt and Meta stay reserved for your browser.

Shift to sprint. C to crouch. No risky Control-based movement.

Protection activates during fullscreen gameplay where supported.

A few things worth knowing

Click or drag to move items. Right-click a stack to split it; right-click with a held stack to place one. Shift-click to move a stack quickly. The recipe book fills a real crafting grid from your inventory.

Make a workbench for 3×3 recipes. Use a pickaxe for stone and ores. Torches make caves safer. Keep your hunger high to heal. A bed sets your respawn point and skips the night.

Creative: open inventory for the full catalog; middle-click to copy a block. F3 diagnostics · F5 save · G field guide.
